DABAR is a quiet Bible devotional and Scripture study companion for Christians who want to bring honest questions to the Bible in everyday life.

Ask questions, reflect on Scripture, and keep a thoughtful journal of what you are learning. DABAR is designed to support personal study with care, humility, and clear boundaries around AI's role.

What you can do in this iOS version:

- Ask Scripture-centered questions in plain language
- Explore biblical context and themes
- Keep a private reflection journal
- Return to past questions and insights
- Read clear AI and doctrine disclosures
- Continue on the free plan without card details

DABAR is not a pastor, priest, counselor, or spiritual authority. It is a study tool that points you back to Scripture and encourages wise pastoral care when needed.
